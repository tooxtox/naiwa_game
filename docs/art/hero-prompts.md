## hero-atlas-v1 — stylized-concept

Create a production-ready transparent 1024×1024 game sprite atlas in an EXACT 4 columns × 4 rows grid of equal 256×256 cells, no grid lines. Use the attached yellow character as identity reference: round tall pear-shaped golden yellow body, huge green eyes with black pupils, small mouth, pale cream oval belly, short olive-brown hands and feet, no tail, no clothes, no weapons. Faithfully preserve recognizable proportions and personality. Stylized softly shaded 3D clay render for a 2.5D overhead action game, camera looking down slightly (about 25 degrees), consistent soft upper-left lighting. All 16 figures have the same body volume and same scale, body height about 196 pixels inside each cell, horizontally centered, feet baseline at local y=240. Full body in every tile, generous clear padding, no floor shadow, no background, true alpha transparency, no text, no border, no scenery. Rows from top to bottom: row 1 front/south view, four sequential subtle walking cycle poses (neutral, left step, neutral recoil, right step); row 2 facing RIGHT/east in three-quarter side view, same four walking poses; row 3 rear/north view, same four walking poses, no face visible; row 4 facing RIGHT/east action poses in order: windup with olive hands pulled back; broad open-palm strike reaching right; low forward dash lean with arms tucked; recoiling hurt with squeezed eyes. Keep green eyes and pale belly visible in front and side rows, not rear row. Design as actual game frames, not a character sheet poster. Do not add accessories, glows, captions, ground shadows, duplicate characters within a cell, checkerboard backdrop, or extra limbs.

## hero-portrait-v1 — stylized-concept

Create one transparent full-body hero cutout, using attached reference as exact character identity. Golden yellow rounded pear-shaped creature, huge green eyes, small mouth, pale cream belly, olive-brown hands and feet; preserve reference proportions and friendly deadpan expression. Turn body slightly toward the viewer, subtle heroic ready pose with hands relaxed beside belly, camera looking down slightly (25 degrees) compatible with an overhead 2.5D fantasy game. Polished softly shaded 3D clay render with warm upper-left highlight, clean readable contour; no costume, armor, weapon or new accessories. Entire body and both feet in frame, centered, with ~10% transparent padding. True alpha transparent background, no floor shadow, no scenery, no text, no watermark. This cutout will be used as a small HUD portrait and character identity art.
